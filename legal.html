Performance measurement by Netlify

Netlify currently adds a small script (/.netlify/scripts/rum) to the page that measures loading performance ("Core Web Vitals": how fast the page appears and responds) together with the page address and general browser and device type. The script is served from this site's own domain, sets no cookies and uses no browser storage, and sends its measurements from your browser to Netlify's collection server (ingesteer.services-prod.nsvcs.net), which therefore also sees your IP address. The site owner only sees aggregate figures. It is not used for advertising and is not combined with other data by this operator.

The AI role-play and the panel debrief

When you use Five Star Training, your browser sends the following to this site's server functions:

WhenWhat is sent
Each turn of the role-play (/api/roleplay)The scenario's dispatch context and customer persona (for "Build Your Own Scenario": the text you entered, up to 1,500 characters each), plus the whole conversation so far, each message up to 1,500 characters.

Neither the server functions nor this operator store the text; nothing is written to a database or log file by the site's own code.

The "Copy transcript & debrief" button only copies text to your device's clipboard. Nothing is uploaded.

AI disclosure and limits

  • The customer is simulated. In Five Star Training you are talking to an AI model instructed to play a customer. Its reactions are plausible, not predictive of any real person.
  • The panel is one model, not three experts. The "Customer", "HVAC Tech" and "Psychologist" in the debrief are three roles written by the same AI model in a single pass. They are not independent reviewers and not statements by Dr. Fassbender.
  • It can be wrong. AI output can be inaccurate, inconsistent between runs, or biased. The debrief is a prompt for reflection and discussion, not a grade, assessment, or certification.
  • For instructors: treat the debrief like a peer's feedback, useful to discuss, and pair it with your own judgment and rubric.
